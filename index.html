How it works

1. Pick a model that fits your device. Smaller models are faster, bigger ones are smarter.
2. Download it once. It's saved in your browser.
3. Chat for free, even offline.

Tip: on phones or older laptops, start with a "Tiny" model under 1.5 GB.

Models are open source and run locally with WebLLM. Answers can be wrong, so check anything important.
